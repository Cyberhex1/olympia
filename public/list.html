          Why sleep instead of shutting down?

          • Sleep keeps your work open and wakes in a couple of seconds.
          • It uses only about 2–5 watts, so a few dollars a year.
          • It doesn't wear out your hardware: fans, drives and the graphics card are all stopped.
          • Desktops also save your work to disk (hybrid sleep), so a power cut doesn't lose it.
          • Your phone can wake the PC over the network (Wake-on-LAN). That works reliably from sleep, not from shutdown.

          Restart about once a week to clear slowdowns.

          Shut down fully when you're away for a long time, a storm is coming, or a laptop could be stolen.
